Let's talk about the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange. If you've been watching the TA-35 lately, you know it's a rollercoaster powered by equal parts geopolitical whiplash and pure startup adrenaline.

While real estate stocks are sweating out the interest rate environment, Israeli tech continues to do what it does best: survive, pivot, and secure bags of capital. The shekel might be dancing around, giving importers a headache, but export-heavy cyber and AI firms are quietly printing cash. 

Bottom line: The local market isn't for the faint of heart right now. It's volatile, it's messy, but the underlying engine of the 'Startup Nation' is still humming. Keep an eye on defense tech and seed-stage AI—that's where the smart money is moving while everyone else is panicking.
