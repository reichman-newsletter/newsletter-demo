Across the pond, Wall Street is officially entering its post-hype hangover. We've spent the last two years hyperventilating over every AI chip announcement, and now the market is finally asking for the receipts. 

The S&P 500 and Nasdaq are looking a bit top-heavy as investors rotate out of the tech heavyweights and start bargain-hunting in small caps and industrials. Meanwhile, the Fed is playing its usual game of poker with interest rates—hinting at cuts just enough to keep traders from jumping ship, but remaining cautious.

The takeaway? The "soft landing" dream is still alive, but the easy money in big tech has already been made. If your portfolio is just a giant bet on GPUs, it might be time to diversify before the music stops.
